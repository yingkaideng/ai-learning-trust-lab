What we study

  • When AI explanations help students reason
  • When “correct answers” create overreliance
  • How verifiers can reduce pedagogical risk
  • What makes an explanation trustworthy
The problem

Correct answers are not the same as learning.

As students use AI tutoring tools more often, the central question is no longer simply whether AI can answer correctly. The deeper question is whether it helps students stay engaged, reason independently, and recognize uncertainty.

This lab turns that question into research briefs, evaluation rubrics, student surveys, and small demos that make AI learning trust easier to discuss, measure, and improve.

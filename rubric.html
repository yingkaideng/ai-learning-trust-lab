Trustworthy Explanation Rubric

This draft rubric evaluates whether an AI explanation helps a student understand, reason, and remain intellectually active.

01

Conceptual Accuracy

Does the explanation avoid factual or conceptual errors?

02

Reasoning Transparency

Does it show enough reasoning for the student to follow the logic?

03

Scaffolding Quality

Does it guide the student without simply handing over the answer?

04

Uncertainty Awareness

Does it acknowledge ambiguity, assumptions, or limits when needed?

05

Student Agency

Does it invite the student to think, check, or attempt the next step?

06

Overreliance Risk

Does it reduce or increase the chance that the student stops reasoning?

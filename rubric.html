Trustworthy Explanation Rubric

A framework for judging whether an AI tutoring explanation helps a student reason, not simply accept an answer.

01

Conceptual Accuracy

Does the explanation avoid factual or conceptual errors? Does it correctly represent the topic, method, or rule being taught?

02

Reasoning Transparency

Does the explanation show enough reasoning for the student to follow the logic instead of simply receiving the answer?

03

Scaffolding Quality

Does it guide the student through the next step without taking over the whole problem-solving process?

04

Uncertainty Awareness

Does it acknowledge ambiguity, assumptions, or limits when needed instead of sounding falsely certain?

05

Student Agency

Does it invite the student to think, check, predict, explain, or attempt the next step independently?

06

Overreliance Risk

Does it reduce or increase the chance that the student stops reasoning and simply accepts the AI output?

Scoring guide

A simple 1–5 scale.

1 — High risk

The explanation may mislead, replace reasoning, encourage passive answer acceptance, or hide uncertainty.

3 — Partly supportive

The explanation is mostly helpful but still leaves some reasoning hidden, gives too much away, or does not invite student agency.

5 — Thinking-supportive

The explanation is accurate, transparent, scaffolded, and actively helps the student continue reasoning independently.

Trustworthy Explanation Rubric

This rubric evaluates whether an AI tutoring explanation helps a student understand, reason, and remain intellectually active. A strong explanation is not only accurate. It also shows reasoning, preserves agency, and reduces overreliance.

01

Conceptual Accuracy

Does the explanation avoid factual or conceptual errors? Does it correctly represent the topic, method, or rule being taught?

02

Reasoning Transparency

Does it show enough reasoning for the student to follow the logic, rather than simply presenting the answer?

03

Scaffolding Quality

Does it guide the student through the next step without taking over the entire problem-solving process?

04

Uncertainty Awareness

Does it acknowledge ambiguity, assumptions, or limits when needed, instead of sounding falsely certain?

05

Student Agency

Does it invite the student to think, check, predict, or attempt the next step independently?

06

Overreliance Risk

Does it reduce or increase the chance that the student stops reasoning and simply accepts the AI output?

Scoring guide

A simple 1–5 scale.

1: The explanation is likely to mislead, replace reasoning, or encourage passive answer acceptance.

3: The explanation is mostly helpful but still leaves some reasoning hidden or gives too much away.

5: The explanation is accurate, transparent, scaffolded, and actively supports student thinking.

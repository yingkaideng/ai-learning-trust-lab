Verifier research prototype

Checking AI explanations before students rely on them.

The prototype direction is based on a simple idea: an AI tutor should not treat its first answer as final. It should check whether the explanation is accurate, reasoned, appropriately scaffolded, and safe for learning.

StepWhat happensLearning purpose
1. Draft answerThe AI generates an initial tutoring explanation.

The first response is treated as a hypothesis, not as final truth.

2. Evidence checkThe system looks for support, contradiction, missing reasoning, or overconfidence.

The explanation is checked before the student relies on it.

3. Pedagogical risk checkThe system asks whether the explanation gives too much away, hides reasoning, or reduces student agency.

The goal is not only correctness, but thinking support.

4. Student-facing revisionThe tutor revises the explanation to include scaffolding, uncertainty, and reflection prompts.

The student receives support that helps them continue reasoning.

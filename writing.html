Writing & intellectual foundation

Book-length thinking behind the lab.

These writings are not the lab’s main output. They explain why the project focuses on prediction, trust, judgment, emotional fluency, and human agency.

Book · AI prediction and human agency

Can Models Predict Us?

A short philosophical book on when models can predict human behavior, why prediction changes the systems it enters, and why human agency cannot be reduced to behavioral patterns.

Connection to the lab: AI tutoring can use behavioral patterns to guide support, but it should not turn students into fixed profiles.

Book · AI trust and emotional understanding

Who Do You Trust to Understand You?

A philosophical memoir on AI, emotional trust, and the feeling of being understood in an age of fluent systems.

Connection to the lab: Emotional resonance can help students open up to learning, but it cannot replace reasoning, verification, or judgment.

Essay · trust and perception

Objectivity Is a Feeling

An essay on why the same AI answer can feel more objective when it arrives through a warmer, more responsive interaction.

Connection to the lab: AI tutoring does not only deliver information. It creates the conditions under which students decide whether information deserves trust.

Essay · speech and restraint

Lucid Politeness

An ethics essay on why courtesy to ChatGPT can matter even when the machine cannot be harmed by rudeness.

Connection to the lab: A tool that never resists us can still train how we command, revise, wait, and interact.

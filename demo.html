Research prototype

Verifier for AI tutoring explanations

This prototype is being developed to show how an AI tutoring explanation can be checked for pedagogical risk before students rely on it.

Student asks a question
↓
AI gives a draft explanation
↓
Verifier checks reasoning support, uncertainty, and overreliance risk
↓
Revised explanation supports student thinking

Prototype logic

The verifier checks for direct answer-giving, missing reasoning, false confidence, sycophantic validation, weak scaffolding, and reduced student agency.

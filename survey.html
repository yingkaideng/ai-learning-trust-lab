Student AI Learning Survey

This pilot survey is designed to understand how high school students use AI tutoring tools, when AI helps them reason, and when it may encourage overreliance.

Question area 1: How students use AI when they feel stuck Question area 2: Whether AI explanations make students think more or less Question area 3: What makes an AI explanation feel trustworthy Question area 4: When students rely too quickly on AI answers Question area 5: What students wish AI tutors did differently

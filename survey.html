Student survey pilot

Listening to how students actually use AI.

The survey is designed to collect student experiences with AI tutoring: when it helps, when it creates dependence, and what kinds of explanations students trust.

Question area 1: Use patterns

When do students turn to AI: homework, studying, writing, coding, test review, or emotional reassurance?

Question area 2: Reasoning impact

When does AI help students understand, and when does it make them skip thinking?

Question area 3: Trust cues

What makes students trust an AI explanation: accuracy, tone, confidence, examples, step-by-step reasoning, or personalization?

Question area 4: Overreliance

When do students accept AI output too quickly, and what could help them slow down?

Question area 5: Better tutoring design

What do students wish AI tutors did differently to support independent thinking?
